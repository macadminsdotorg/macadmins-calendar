---
title: "Submit an Event"
url: "/submit/"
---

Suggest a conference, meetup, or community event for the calendar. The preview shows exactly how it will appear as you type. When it looks right, continue to GitHub to send it; a Mac Admins Foundation volunteer reviews every submission before it goes live.

Good fits are **broadly relevant to the Mac admin community, offer high-quality technical or professional content, are live (in person or virtual), and welcome all attendees.** Sales pitches, invite-only events, and pre-recorded events aren't a fit.
